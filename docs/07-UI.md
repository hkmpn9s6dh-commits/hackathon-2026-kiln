# 07 · UI decision record

Four sections, as the house rule requires: **style preset, rulers, one
memorable point, main-screen spec.** Written before the art, corrected after the
first capture (the corrections are marked ⟲ so the record stays honest).

---

## 1. Style preset

> **A night shift at a wood kiln, photographed.** One warm light source, deep
> brick dark, nothing on screen that is not the kiln, the pot, or the fire.

| Decision | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light | one source, low and left (the firebox) | Everything in a real kiln is lit by the fire. Two light sources would make it a dashboard. |
| Ground | `#100c09`, warming to `#1d1009` at the floor | The page is the inside of a brick chamber, not a card on white. Reddit's dark mode is the default here, not an afterthought. |
| Heat ramp | `#1a0f0a → #4a1d0e → #8c2f0e → #c9451a → #ef7a25 → #f7b455 → #ffe6a8 → #fffaf0` | Eight stops, used for the field, the flame, and every number that means temperature. One ramp, everywhere. |
| Paper | `#ece0cb` with ink `#241a14` | The result is a page from a 瓷谱, not a modal. The only light surface in the app, so the eye goes there. |
| Accent | vermilion `#b8442a` (seal), celadon `#9fc9b8` (good), gold `#f0b45c` (streak / "you") | Three accents, each with a job. Never decorative. |
| Display type | serif (Iowan / Palatino / Songti fallback), letterspaced small caps for the wordmark | Clay and print, not tech. |
| Data type | monospace, 10–11.5px, letterspaced | Numbers are instrument readings; they should look read, not written. |
| Motion | only two: flames (continuous, additive) and the door-opening reveal (1.1s, clip-path) | The kiln moves; the interface does not. |
| Density | one decision per screen; never two buttons of equal weight | A daily game is 60 seconds; a second call to action is a tax. |

⟲ Correction after capture 1: the first heat field used a boolean "in band"
tint, which drew a hard diagonal edge across the kiln and read as a bug. Changed
to a squared falloff (`glow²`) plus a gamma on the ramp. Same information, no
seam.

⟲ Correction after capture 2: the first fire scene was a pot floating in a
black rectangle. Added the chamber arch, the settler slab under the pot, an
ember bed, and a warm rim light on the pot's fire side. The pot now stands
*somewhere*.

## 2. Rulers (≥5, each with the one thing borrowed)

None of these is a visual reference to copy. Each is a rule that survives being
moved into a Reddit post.

| # | Work | What we take | What we deliberately do differently |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Wordle** (NYT) | The share line: one glance, no spoilers, social currency. A result people can post without ruining the puzzle. | Our share is one line of text, not a coloured grid — the pot itself is the mnemonic, and the grid would give away the schedule. |
| 2 | **NYT Connections** | Category colour as information: a small, fixed palette where hue *means* something. | We use a continuous ramp instead of four categories, because our variable is temperature, not classification. |
| 3 | **Monument Valley** | One shape language and one accent; calm negative space around the interactive thing. | Our negative space is dark and warm rather than pastel; a kiln at night is not a meditation app. |
| 4 | **Papers, Please** | The interface *is* the fiction: stamps, forms, a desk. Nothing is a generic widget. | We have no text to read under time pressure; the equivalent is the readout, which is one line and always in the same place. |
| 5 | **A Little to the Left** | Physical satisfaction in small motions: the object responds to the hand, and alignment feels good before it is scored. | We cannot rely on drag-and-drop tidiness alone; the kiln's heat field has to make the correct spot *visible*, or the satisfaction has no target. |
| 6 | **r/place** (Reddit) | Collective action made visible on one shared canvas; the artefact of the crowd is the content. | Ours is per-day and per-subreddit, so the canvas is small enough to read in one screen and resets — a daily kiln, not a monument. |
| 7 | **Reigns** (Nerial) | A single continuous gesture as the whole input; the UI disappears behind one physical verb. | Our verb is hold-to-feed, not swipe; the feedback is thermal and slow rather than instant and binary. |

Transferable rules extracted (these are what actually shaped the build):

1. **The result must be postable in one line.** (Wordle)
2. **Colour must carry meaning, not decoration.** (Connections, r/place)
3. **One light, one shape language, generous dark.** (Monument Valley)
4. **The interface is the fiction; no generic widgets on the hero screen.** (Papers, Please)
5. **Small physical motions must feel good before they are scored.** (A Little to the Left)
6. **The crowd has to be visible while the decision is being made, not after.** (r/place)
7. **One continuous gesture beats three buttons.** (Reigns)

## 3. The one memorable point

> **The pot's colour is the score. You can read the firing off the object before
> you read the number.**

Everything else is in service of that: a single warm light so glaze colour is
legible; a paper card so the object sits alone; the verdict seal small and to
the side, so the eye hits the pot first. Underfired goes chalky and pale;
overfired goes brown and runs at the foot; a flambé goes red under green,
mottled, and cannot be reproduced.

Consequence for the build: the pot renderer takes the *actual* heat and verdict
as input (`PotPaint.mood`), and the same renderer draws the thumbnail in the feed
card, the pot on the map, the pot in the fire, and the pot on the card — so the
object a player recognises is the object that gets graded.

## 4. Main-screen spec

**Frames.** Designed at 390 (phone), 620 (the column max), 820 (desktop
expanded). The game column is always centred, `max-width: 620px`, page padding
14/16. No horizontal scroll at any width ≥ 320.

**Vertical order (load screen).**

| Block | Height | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top bar | 28 | wordmark left, date + reset centre-right, streak/name right. Mono 11px. |
| Ware card | 96 | 74px pot thumbnail, name (serif 17), Chinese name (serif 12, 0.18em), body hint (mono 10.5), ideal heat right-aligned. |
| Kiln | 4:2.33 canvas, full width | The hero. Nothing overlaps it except the readout and the hint pill. |
| Action | 56 | One primary button. Disabled until a spot is chosen; its sub-label names the shelf. |
| Caption | ~3 lines | 10.5px mono, 44% opacity. Explains the crowd in one sentence, never more. |

**Type scale.** 42 (score) / 23 (card title) / 20 (wordmark) / 17 (ware) / 16
(button) / 14 (body) / 13.5 (notes) / 11.5 (data) / 10.5 (captions). Nothing
between steps.

**Hit targets.** ≥ 44px for the fire button (it is 60), ≥ 44px for the card's
share button, and the whole kiln canvas is the placement target.

**Readout (top-right of the kiln).** Three lines, always in this order:
verdict + estimated score (`LAND TRUE · 91`), heat vs band (`heat 0.57 · band
0.50–0.60`), crowd (`3 pots on this shelf · 12 in the kiln`). 10.5px mono on
`rgba(12,8,6,.82)` with a 1px warm border. It never covers the band line for
long: it is 132px wide on a 620px frame and the band runs diagonally.

**States.**

- *empty kiln* — no numbers in the corners, hint reads "drag or tap inside the kiln";
- *crowded* — a 22×16 pill with the count in the shelf's top-right; the field
  under a crowded shelf is visibly duller;
- *already fired* — the load screen is skipped entirely; the card is shown with
  the stored result and the board;
- *error* — one sentence and a `Try again` button; never a stack trace;
- *loading* — the wordmark and "lighting the kiln…", nothing else.

**Accessibility.**

- Contrast: body text ≥ 4.5:1 on its own ground; captions are the one deliberate
  exception at ~3.5:1 and never carry information that exists nowhere else.
- The fire is playable with `Space` (keydown/keyup) as well as pointer hold.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` removes the door animation and the flame easing.
- Colour is never the only signal: every verdict also prints 合格/精品/过火/生烧/
  惊釉/窑变 plus its English name, and the score is a number.
- Text is real text (DOM), not baked into canvas, so it scales with the browser.

⟲ Correction after capture 3: the kiln hint was set directly on the heat field
and disappeared against orange. It is now a dark pill, bottom-left, always in
the same place.
